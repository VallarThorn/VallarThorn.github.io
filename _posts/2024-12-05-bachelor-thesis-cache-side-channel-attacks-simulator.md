My Bachelor's thesis in Computer Science at **Graz University of Technology**, written at the
**Institute of Applied Information Processing and Communications (IAIK)** under the supervision of
**Daniel Gruss** and **Lukas Giner**.

## Abstract

Side channels have become a significant area of study in information security and have led to
important breakthroughs in the development of new techniques and exploits. This thesis contributes
to a deeper understanding of side channels in the cache, and extends a cache simulator to help
discover and develop new advanced cache attacks.

It describes the implementation of a **multicore cache simulator with directory-based coherence**
(MSI/MOESI), evaluates the performance of different architectures, and finally runs a
**Prime+Probe** side-channel attack against the new simulator.
